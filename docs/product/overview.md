# Product Overview

## What it is
hevy-garmin-mcp — an MCP server that gives AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) direct, real-time access to a personal athlete's training and performance data from Garmin Connect and Hevy.

## Problem it solves
AI coaching tools have no memory of your training history and no access to your actual data. Every session starts from scratch — you manually paste screenshots and numbers, and the AI forgets everything next time.

## Core value proposition
Connect once, and your AI coach always has full context: your runs, recovery, lifting sessions, and trends — without you having to feed it anything manually.

## Success criteria (6 months)
The AI coach gives personalized plans and feedback that are genuinely relevant to the athlete's current fitness state, and those recommendations have measurably helped reach training goals.
