# Users

## Primary users
A single hobby athlete (the developer). Uses ChatGPT and Claude regularly for training advice, tracks runs and cycling on Garmin Connect, and logs strength training in Hevy.

## Key pain points
- Manually copying training data (screenshots, numbers) into AI tools before every coaching conversation
- AI loses all context between sessions — no memory of past training, goals, or patterns
- Hard to get genuinely personalized advice without spending 5 minutes re-briefing the AI each time

## Who feels the pain most
The athlete mid-training-cycle who wants to ask a quick coaching question ("should I run hard today given my HRV?") but has to manually provide all context to get a useful answer.
